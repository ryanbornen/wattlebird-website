Tankwise

Aquarium water, made simple · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Log a water test in a few taps straight from your test kit's colour chart. Tankwise tells you what each number means, what to do about it, and when a new tank is safe for fish.

Features

  • Tap-to-log: chips that match the colour-chart steps on common liquid test kits
  • Every reading marked OK, keep an eye or act now, with plain-English advice
  • Ranges for tropical, coldwater, planted and shrimp, and marine tanks
  • Nitrogen cycle tracker for new tanks, from the ammonia stage and the nitrite spike to "safe to add fish"
  • Water change countdown, and a calculator that tells you how much to change
  • Works offline. No account, no ads.
  • Free for 1 tank, with every feature
  • Pro (A$9.99 once): unlimited tanks, CSV export, and backup and restore

Tankwise gives general fishkeeping guidance. Some species need different water, so check what yours prefer.
