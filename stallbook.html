Stall Book

For market stallholders · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Markets, applications, stall fees, packing lists and takings, all in one place. Stall Book tells you what to do next and shows you which markets are actually worth your weekend.

Features

  • Every market date from "thinking about it" to applied, accepted, paid and done
  • A to-do list: apply by, pay by, pack, and log how it went
  • A packing list you tick off for each market day
  • Log cash and card takings, with a cash-tin counter that takes out your float
  • Profit after stall fees and other costs, and profit per hour
  • Warnings before your public liability insurance or permits run out, including if they run out before a market you've booked
  • Results by Australian financial year
  • Works offline. No account, no ads.
  • Free: up to 3 markets. Pro (A$7.99 once): unlimited markets, markets ranked by profit, spreadsheet export and backup.

Profit figures are before stock and materials. Stall Book isn't tax advice.
