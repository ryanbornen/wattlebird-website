Rack Coach

Train for crossword board games · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Good players aren't magic. They know their short words and they see the best play on a rack. Rack Coach trains both, a few minutes a day.

Features

  • Word or not? Quick drills with believable fakes, so you stop playing phoneys
  • Spaced review: words you miss come back tomorrow, words you know come back less often
  • Decks for two- and three-letter words, Q without U, short J, X and Z words, and vowel dumps
  • Rack drill: seven tiles, find your best play, then see what you missed
  • Day streak and words learned. Works offline, no account, no ads.
  • Free: the two-letter deck. Pro (A$7.99 once): every deck and the rack drill.

Rack Coach isn't affiliated with any word game or its makers. It uses a general English dictionary, not an official tournament list. Word list from SCOWL, © 2000–2011 Kevin Atkinson and others, used under its licence.
