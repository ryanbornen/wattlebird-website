ReportReady

Inspection checklists and PDF reports · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Walk the site, tap OK, Issue or N/A for each item, add a note and photo where something's wrong, and leave with a finished PDF report. No more typing up reports at night.

Features

  • Ready-made checklists: rental property condition, site safety walk, vehicle pre-start and general maintenance
  • OK, Issue or N/A for each item, with notes and up to 4 photos
  • Saves as you go, so nothing is lost if you get interrupted
  • Track fixes: mark each issue fixed when it's done, even after the report is sent. The list shows what's still to fix.
  • Finished reports lock to match what you sent. If you reopen one, it becomes a new revision.
  • PDF report with a summary, the issues found up top, then every section with photos
  • Your business name and ABN on every report
  • Works offline. Everything stays on your phone, with no account and no cloud.
  • Free: 3 inspections a month with the built-in checklists
  • Pro (A$12.99 a month or A$99.99 a year): unlimited inspections, your own checklists, voice notes, CSV export, and backup and restore

Built-in checklists are general guides. ReportReady reports are visual inspection records, not certificates of compliance with any law or standard.
