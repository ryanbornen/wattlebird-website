ProofShot

Job photo logs for builders and tradies · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Snap before, progress and finished photos on every job. ProofShot records when and where each one was taken, then turns them into a tidy proof-of-work report for the client, the builder or the insurer. Settle disputes and get paid without the back and forth.

Features

  • Jobs with client, site address and job or quote number
  • Tag photos as Before, Progress, Finished, Defect, Variation, Delivery or Safety
  • Date, time and location recorded with every entry, with the street name
  • Photos picked from the library are clearly marked, so reports stay honest
  • PDF report with your business name, ABN and licence, a log table and every photo
  • Works offline. Everything stays on your phone, with no account and no cloud.
  • Free: 1 active job, reports up to 12 photos
  • Pro (A$9.99 a month or A$79.99 a year): unlimited jobs, full reports, voice notes, CSV export, and backup and restore
