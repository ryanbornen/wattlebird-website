Privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Applies to PrintLedger and other apps by Wattlebird Software

The short version: PrintLedger keeps your printers, spools and print records on your own device. We don't collect, see, sell or share that information.

Who we are

Wattlebird Software is a business name registered in Australia to Ryan Bornen (ABN 16 538 424 771). In this policy, "we" and "us" means Wattlebird Software. Contact: hello@wattlebirdsoftware.com.au.

Information stored in the app

The details you enter into PrintLedger (printer names, filament spools, print jobs, costs, notes and settings) are stored only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to any server we operate. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Delete all data, removes them.

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or device identifiers through the app. The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Purchases

If you buy PrintLedger Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card details. To check whether you have Pro, the app may use a purchase-management service (RevenueCat), which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records from the store, not your name or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Exporting and sharing

If you export your data as CSV, the app hands it to your device's share menu. Where it goes from there (email, cloud storage and so on) is your choice and is governed by that service.

Emailing us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply and help you. We keep support emails only as long as needed and never sell or share them.

Children

Our apps are general-audience tools and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. You can ask us what information we hold about you (normally none, apart from any emails you've sent) or ask us to correct or delete it by emailing hello@wattlebirdsoftware.com.au. If you're unhappy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If an app starts handling information differently (for example, adding cloud backup), we'll update this page and the effective date before that change is released.
