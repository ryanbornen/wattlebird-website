ShutterPlan

Shot lists and light planning for photographers · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Never miss the shot that matters. Start from a ready-made shot list, mark the must-haves, tick them off on the day, and know exactly when golden hour hits at your location.

Features

  • Shot list templates for weddings, portraits, events, real estate and products
  • Mark must-have shots and see which are still to get
  • Sunrise, sunset, golden hour and blue hour for any location and date, worked out on your phone
  • Scouting spots saved with GPS, notes and photos
  • Share the shot list with a second shooter or client
  • Works offline. Everything stays on your phone.
  • Free: 2 upcoming shoots and 1 scouting spot
  • Pro (A$3.99 a month or A$29.99 a year): unlimited shoots and spots, scouting photos, PDF shot lists and backup
