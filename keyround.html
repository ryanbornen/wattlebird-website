KeyRound

Rounds, keys and visit times for dog walkers, pet sitters and cleaners · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Your clients line up as today's round. Tap "I'm here" and "Finished here", and KeyRound times the visit and writes the update for the client. It also keeps track of who has which key.

Features

  • Regular weekly or fortnightly visits fill your round automatically. Reorder, skip or add extras.
  • Time-stamped visits, with one-tap updates to the client that double as proof of visit
  • Key register: with you, in a lockbox, with the client, or with someone else. "Keys with you" shows on your round.
  • Gate, lockbox and alarm codes hidden until tapped. Pet and house notes on every visit.
  • Week view: visits, time on site and what you've earned, with a visit list to send each client
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, and your clients' codes never leave your phone.
  • Free: up to 5 active clients
  • Pro (A$4.99 a month or A$39.99 a year): unlimited clients, PDF visit statements, and backup and restore
