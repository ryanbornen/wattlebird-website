BoardSaver

Cut lists for timber and sheet goods · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Type in your parts and what the hardware store sells. BoardSaver works out the fewest sheets or lengths to buy, draws where every part goes, and gives you the cut order, so you spend less and waste less.

Features

  • Sheets (ply, MDF, melamine) and lengths (pine, DAR, studs, decking) in metric Australian sizes
  • Allows for your saw kerf, keeps grain direction where you need it
  • Buy list with the total cost, using the prices you enter
  • Uses the offcuts already in your shed first
  • Easy savings: spots a part that is a couple of millimetres too big and costs you a whole extra sheet
  • Cut order by fence setting, and tap each part as it's cut
  • Edge banding total, with 10% extra for trimming
  • Share the plan as text, or print a cutting-plan PDF with diagrams
  • Works offline in the shed. No account, no ads
  • Free: 2 projects, 12 parts each
  • Pro (A$12.99 once, no subscription): unlimited projects and parts, PDF cutting plans, backup

Plans are a guide. Check your measurements and stock sizes before cutting.
