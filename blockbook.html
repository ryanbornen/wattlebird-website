BlockBook

Livestock records for hobby farms and lifestyle blocks · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

A few cattle, a mob of sheep, a couple of goats. Big farm software is overkill and expensive, and a notebook doesn't tell you when a drench is due or when an animal is clear to sell. BlockBook does.

Features

  • Every animal with tag, sex, age, paddock, weights and full history. Add a whole mob in one go.
  • Withholding periods worked out from the label: meat, milk and export slaughter interval, with a warning before you sell or slaughter too early
  • Paddocks: who's where, how long each one has rested, and which are ready to graze
  • Drench, vaccine and booster reminders, with "same as last time" to fill in the details
  • Treat everyone in a paddock in a few taps, with batch numbers and who gave it
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, no camera or location access.
  • Free: up to 12 animals
  • Pro (A$7.99 a month or A$59.99 a year): unlimited animals, a records PDF with your treatment record, CSV export, and backup and restore

BlockBook is a record-keeping aid, not veterinary advice. It doesn't connect to NLIS. Always check withholding periods on the product label.
