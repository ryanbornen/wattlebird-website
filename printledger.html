PrintLedger

3D printing job, filament and cost tracker · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Know exactly what every print costs. PrintLedger adds up filament, electricity and extras, keeps track of what's left on each spool, and suggests a selling price, which is ideal for Etsy sellers and anyone printing to order.

Features

  • Log prints with grams, print time and extras, and see the real cost instantly
  • Suggested selling price using your own markup
  • Spool tracking that subtracts filament automatically, with low-spool warnings and one-tap "bought another like this"
  • Finished spools are put away but kept, so old prints keep their true cost (and they don't count toward the free limit)
  • Monthly totals, success rate and money lost to failed prints
  • Works offline. Your data stays on your device.
  • Photos and voice notes on every print
  • Pro (A$14.99 once): unlimited printers and spools, PDF customer quotes and cost reports, CSV export, and backup and restore
