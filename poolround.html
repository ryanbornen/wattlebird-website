Pool Round

Pool service reports for pool technicians · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Test the water, see exactly what to add, tick off the jobs and send the customer a tidy report before you leave the pool.

Features

  • Today's round: pools due today, visits in progress, and reports still to send
  • Readings for chlorine, pH, alkalinity, hardness, stabiliser, salt and temperature, checked against ideal ranges for each pool
  • Chemical doses worked out from the pool size, with one tap to add them to the report
  • Job checklist: skim, baskets, brush, vacuum, filter, salt cell, pump, water level
  • Customer report as text, or a branded PDF with photos (Pro)
  • Full history for every pool
  • Works offline. No account, no per-tech fees on the solo plan.
  • Free for up to 3 pools. Pro: A$29 a month or A$290 a year.

Dose figures are estimates from standard pool chemistry. Always follow the product label and the equipment maker's advice.
