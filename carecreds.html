CareCreds

Credentials wallet for disability and aged care support workers · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Your NDIS Worker Screening Check, Working With Children Check, first aid, CPR, licence and insurance, all in one place. CareCreds reminds you before anything runs out, so you never get taken off the roster for an expired check.

Features

  • All the common support worker credentials ready to pick, or add your own
  • Photos of each card or certificate, front and back
  • Suggested expiry dates from the issue date (first aid 3 years, CPR 1 year), always editable
  • Reminders 60, 30 and 7 days before expiry, and on the day. You choose.
  • See what's expired or due soon at a glance
  • Applied to renew? Mark it as renewing. The countdown stops, and we remind you to add the new date when the card arrives.
  • Works offline. Everything stays on your phone, with no account and no cloud.
  • Free: up to 4 credentials, reminders included
  • Pro (A$2.99 a month or A$24.99 a year): unlimited credentials, a share pack PDF with photos for new providers and audits, CSV export, and backup and restore

CareCreds is not affiliated with the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission or any state screening agency. Always check renewal rules with your employer and your state.
