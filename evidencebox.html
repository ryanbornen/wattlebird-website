Evidence Box

NDIS audit readiness for small providers · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Know where you stand before the auditor arrives. Evidence Box keeps your policies and records, worker checks and incident deadlines in one place, with a readiness score you can act on.

Features

  • Evidence register with a starter list of 22 common items, review dates and where each one is kept
  • Worker compliance: screening checks, Orientation Module and first aid, with expiry warnings
  • Reportable incident deadlines with live countdowns: the 24-hour notice and 5-day report, plus the unauthorised restrictive practice rules
  • Audit readiness score and countdown to your verification or certification audit
  • Audit evidence pack PDF (Pro)
  • Works offline. Records stay on your phone.
  • Free: evidence register, incident deadlines and 3 workers. Pro: A$49 a month or A$490 a year.

Evidence Box is a record-keeping aid, not legal advice. Always check the NDIS Commission's current rules for your registration groups.
