JargonGym

Learn the lingo of your job · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

New job, new words. JargonGym teaches the vocabulary of a profession in 3-minute daily drills, with plain-English meanings and spaced repetition, so the terms actually stick.

Features

  • NDIS and disability support pack (50 terms, free): SIL, SDA, LAC, core supports, restrictive practices and more
  • Aged care pack (35 terms): Support at Home, SIRS, IDDSI, sundowning, advance care directives and more
  • Building site basics pack (37 terms): SWMS, noggins, lintels, practical completion and more
  • 10-term daily drills that bring back the words you miss and space out the ones you know
  • Streaks, and progress from new to learning, reviewing and mastered
  • Works offline. No account, no ads.
  • Pro (A$5.99 a month or A$39.99 a year): every pack, new packs as they arrive, and backup

Definitions are plain-English learning aids, not legal or clinical advice. Always follow your workplace's policies.
