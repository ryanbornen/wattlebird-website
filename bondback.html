Bond Back

Condition reports for renters · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

When you move out, the condition report you did on day one decides how much of your bond comes back. Bond Back walks you through every room, keeps dated photos with each note, and turns it into a clear PDF you can send to your agent or landlord.

Features

  • Ready-made rooms and items: walls, floors, doors, windows, appliances and more
  • Tick clean, undamaged and working for each item, or tap "All fine here" for a whole room
  • Add a note and photos wherever something isn't right
  • Moving-in, routine and moving-out reports for each rental
  • Shows when your entry report is due (in NSW, within 7 days of moving in)
  • Compare moving in with moving out to see what was already there
  • Share a dated PDF. Sent reports are locked so the record stays as it was.
  • Works offline. No account, no ads.
  • Free: one rental and up to 3 photos per room. Pro (A$9.99 once): unlimited rentals and photos, plus the comparison PDF.

Bond Back helps you keep a good record. It isn't legal advice. For bond disputes, contact your state's fair trading or tenancy body.
