Fresh Sheets

Short-stay turnovers for hosts and cleaners · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Every changeover done right, and proven. Tick through each room, snap proof photos, log damage and low supplies, and send the host a "ready for guests" report.

Features

  • A room-by-room checklist for each property, set once and editable
  • Countdown to the next check-in on every turnover
  • Proof photos for each room
  • Linen count worked out from the beds and guests
  • Damage, maintenance and lost property, plus a restock list for the host
  • Host report as text, or a PDF with every room's photos (Pro)
  • Access details hidden until tapped. Works offline.
  • Free for 1 property. Pro: A$19 a month or A$190 a year, for up to 3 properties.
