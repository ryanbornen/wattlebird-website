Brood Book

Hive records and varroa tests for Australian beekeepers · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

In NSW, every hive needs a varroa test at least every 16 weeks, and every result has to be reported, even zero mites. Brood Book keeps track of each hive's test clock, tells you what still needs reporting, and makes logging an inspection a one-minute job.

Features

  • A to-do list, most urgent first: tests due or overdue, results to report, queenless hives and low stores
  • Quick inspections: queen, eggs, brood pattern, temper, frames of bees, stores, pests and what you did
  • Mite tests by alcohol wash, soapy water, sugar shake or strip, with mites per 100 bees and a guide level
  • NSW reporting reminders: same day if you found mites, within 7 days if you didn't, with a summary ready to paste
  • Queen marking colour by year, hive status (queenless, requeened, died, sold or combined) and honey per hive
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, no camera or location access.
  • Free: up to 2 hives
  • Pro (A$29.99 a year): unlimited hives, a hive register PDF, CSV export, and backup and restore

Brood Book is a record-keeping aid. It doesn't send anything to DPI. Always follow your state's current biosecurity rules.
