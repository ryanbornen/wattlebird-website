Carry Over

Shift handover notes for support workers · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Hand over every shift in a couple of minutes, so the next worker knows exactly where things are at. Priorities go first, every person gets an update, and any job you didn't finish carries over to the next shift automatically.

Features

  • Set up each house or client once, with the people there and any "always show" alerts
  • Handover sections for priorities, each person's mood and update, appointments, the house, and money, keys and car
  • Jobs carry over: tick what you finished, and the rest rolls into the next handover
  • Read confirmation: on a shared house phone, the next worker taps "I've read this". Their name and the time are recorded, so nothing important gets missed.
  • Share the handover as text to the house group chat, email or communication book
  • History of every handover, by house
  • Works offline. Everything stays on your phone, with no account and no cloud.
  • Free: 1 house and 4 handovers a week
  • Pro (A$4.99 a month or A$39.99 a year): unlimited houses and handovers, voice, PDF and CSV export, and backup and restore

Carry Over flags medication and incidents for the next shift but doesn't replace your provider's medication chart or incident reporting.
