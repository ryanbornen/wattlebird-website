Cash Tin

Sales and stock for market stalls · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Ring up sales with one tap, cash or card, and know at pack-up exactly what you took, what should be in the tin, and what you actually made after the stall fee.

Features

  • Tap products to build a sale, then Cash or Card
  • Stock goes down as you sell, with running-low warnings
  • Day total: takings, cash vs card, cost of goods, stall fee and real profit
  • Cash in the tin: float plus cash sales, checked against what you count
  • Best sellers for every market day
  • Works offline, so a bad signal at the market doesn't matter
  • Free: 12 products and 3 market days
  • Pro (A$14.99 once, no subscription): unlimited, CSV export and backup

Cash Tin records sales. It doesn't take card payments: use your own card reader and tap Card.
