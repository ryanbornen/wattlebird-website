Purl & Tally

Row counter for knitters and crocheters · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Count rows with one big tap, keep track of pattern repeats, and get a nudge at the row where something changes. Pick up any project exactly where you left off.

Features

  • One big tap to count, with undo for slips
  • Extra counters for repeats, decreases or anything else, linked to rows or counted separately
  • Pattern repeats: "row 3 of 8, repeat 5" at a glance
  • Row reminders, like "start decreases at row 40"
  • Yarn, needles or hook, pattern and notes for every project
  • Keeps the screen on while you count
  • Free: 2 projects on the go, 1 extra counter each
  • Pro (A$7.99 once, no subscription): unlimited projects and counters, progress photos, voice notes and backup
