PrintLedger FAQ

Where is my data stored?
Only on your device. We don't have an account system or servers holding your print data.

I changed phones and lost my data.
Because data stays on your device, it doesn't move automatically yet. Before switching phones, use CSV export (Pro) to keep a copy. Backup and restore is on our list.

How is the cost worked out?
Filament cost = grams used × (spool price ÷ spool size). Electricity = printer watts ÷ 1000 × print hours × your price per kWh. Extras are added on top. The suggested price is the total cost plus your markup percentage.

How do I restore a Pro purchase?
Purchases are tied to your Google Play (or Apple) account. Reinstall the app while signed in to the same account, and contact us if Pro doesn't come back.

How do I cancel a subscription?
On Android: Google Play app → profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?
Open the app → Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.
