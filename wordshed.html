Word Shed

Word finder and helpers for word games · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Stuck with a rack of awkward letters, or down to your last guess on the daily puzzle? Word Shed keeps a few handy tools in one place, and works with no connection at all.

Features

  • Word finder: every word your letters make, with blanks, patterns and letter scores
  • Five-letter helper for daily guessing games: enter the colours, see what's still possible and the best next guess
  • Score calculator for crossword board games: premium squares, blanks and the all-tiles bonus
  • Scoresheet for 2 to 4 players with whose turn, running totals and undo (Pro)
  • Over 110,000 English words, offline. No account, no ads.
  • Pro (A$9.99 once): every finder result, scoresheets and game history

Word Shed isn't affiliated with any word game or its makers. It uses a general English dictionary, not an official tournament list. Word list from SCOWL, © 2000–2011 Kevin Atkinson and others, used under its licence.
