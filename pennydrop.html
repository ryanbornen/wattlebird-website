Penny Drop

Learn cryptic crosswords, one clue a day · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

Cryptic crosswords look impossible until someone shows you the trick. Then the penny drops. Penny Drop gives you one original clue a day, hints that teach you how it works, and a step-by-step explanation every time.

Features

  • One new clue every day, the same for everyone, so you can compare with friends
  • Two hints: first the clue type and the definition, then the signpost word and first letter
  • Every answer explained step by step
  • Learn all nine clue types: anagrams, hidden words, reversals, charades, containers and more
  • Streaks, stars and a spoiler-free result to share
  • Works offline. No account, no ads.
  • Free: today's clue and the Learn guide. Pro (A$3.99 a month or A$29.99 a year): practise every clue by type.

Every clue is original, written for Penny Drop.
