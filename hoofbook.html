Hoofbook

Horse care and costs for Australian owners · Android (coming soon), iPhone and iPad later

When was the farrier last out? Is worming due? What did the dentist say? Hoofbook keeps every visit for every horse, tells you what's coming up, and gives your agistment yard or a sitter everything they need to know.

Features

  • Farrier, worming, dentist and vaccination reminders on intervals you set
  • Bookings that clear themselves when you log the visit
  • Log one farrier visit for several horses and the cost is split for you
  • A horse profile with feed plan, handling alerts, microchip, and vet and farrier contacts, ready to share
  • Costs: farrier and vet bills add up automatically, plus feed, agistment and gear, per month and per horse
  • Works offline. No account, no ads, no camera or location access.
  • Free: one horse with care records, bookings and reminders
  • Pro (A$9.99 a month or A$79.99 a year): unlimited horses, costs, profile sharing, a records PDF, and backup and restore

Hoofbook is a record-keeping aid, not veterinary advice. Ask your vet and farrier what schedule suits your horse.
